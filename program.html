The student program

Selected high school students live and study together for 18 months alongside their final school years.

What scholars receive

  • A fully funded 18-month residential program
  • Life skills and leadership modules
  • Law college preparation and CLAT coaching
  • 11th–12th class curriculum

After the program

  • Guidance on college admissions and scholarships
  • On-campus support once at university
  • Help with internships and careers
  • Lifelong need-based assistance

Recruitment

Students are recruited from over 150 selected districts across India.

Cohorts

An earlier listing described a cohort of 30 students preparing for CLAT, AILET and other law entrance tests in the 2022–23 academic year. Current cohort sizes may differ.

NLSP also uses volunteer and intern task forces. Some members serve as personal or group mentors and help onboard and orient new scholars.
